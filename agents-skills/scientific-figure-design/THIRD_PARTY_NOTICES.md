# Third-party notices

This repository bundles third-party SVG icons so the figure-authoring pipeline
works offline and produces portable editable Draw.io files.

The imported 512-icon collection and its curation are derived from
[Mizar77/ml-paper-icons](https://github.com/Mizar77/ml-paper-icons), whose
original scripts, gallery, curation, and documentation are MIT licensed.

The complete upstream license notices are preserved in:

- `assets/paper-icons/LICENSE`
- `assets/paper-icons/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`
- `assets/paper-icons/library/license-manifest.json`

The 37 project-original icons under `assets/paper-icons/library/svg/` are
released under this repository's MIT license. Their PNG renderings are provided
for preview and compatibility; SVG remains the preferred source.

Brand logo SVG code is redistributed under the Lobe Icons MIT license, but the
logos and names remain trademarks of their respective owners. The software
license does not grant trademark rights.

Use a brand logo only for accurate academic identification or comparison:

- do not imply endorsement or affiliation;
- preserve the official colors encoded in the catalog;
- follow the owner's brand guidance where applicable;
- prefer a neutral text label or generic academic icon when identification does
  not require the logo.
